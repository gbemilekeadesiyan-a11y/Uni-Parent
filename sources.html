Method, not mystery

How Uni Parent makes a plan.

The schedule is produced by visible, deterministic rules. No AI model decides where your time goes.

On this pagePlanning logicRecommendationsPrivacyLimitations

Planning logic

The planner validates the waking window and same-day commitments, rejects overlaps, subtracts fixed blocks, and ranks the remaining windows. It then attempts one study block, one career action, wellbeing time, and editable leisure time. Anything that cannot fit is reported as unscheduled.

Review your commitments or inspect today’s generated calendar.

Recommendation boundaries

Major area changes examples and study wording. Age band changes transparent wellbeing defaults. Neither removes career tracks, predicts ability, or assigns a stereotyped workload. Unknown inputs receive a general-purpose action.

Privacy and storage

Profile, schedule, generated plans, and completion state remain in browser localStorage. The site has no account, tracking script, server database, or live AI request.

Limitations

The current version expects wake and sleep times on the same calendar day. Weekly commitments are entered manually, career actions come from a small reviewed catalog, and recommendations are planning suggestions rather than medical, academic, or professional guarantees.

The implementation and automated checks are documented in the public source repository.
